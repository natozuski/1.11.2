if i was going to build a human from scratch, this is how i would do it
step 1: the brain
step 2: the nerves
stretch the nerves out from the brain; now you have a brain-jellyfish.
step 3: the bones
this is an important part. now you must institute the bones, all 206 of them (3 in each inner ear; 26 in each foot; 27 in each hand etc etc.) the bones will attach to each other using ligaments and theres lovely cartilage on the end of the bones. the little pockets that the meeting ends of bone will eventually create will be filled with lovely synovial oil.
the spine will house the nerves and each vertebrae will have nerves sticking out of it.
lovely content within the blood will come out of the bones so we mustnt forget them.
step 4: muscles
get the other organs, like liver and stomache and all that, and place them near the bones where theyre supposed to go.
you have to put them in in the right order, so simultaneously you get the muscles, wrap them in epimysium, then stretch them out and stick them to the bones and suck them into the bone using tendons. the nerves will pour over and through the epimysium, and theres space there so the blood will eventually do the same.
its about 400 muscles. some of them will wrap over the organs like how the abdominal muscles will weave over the stomache like some sort of plaid weave or something
cant forget about the heart either. the blood reaches all parts of the body through blood vessels and the heart makes sure that there is a bloodflow in all those vessels, because it has to become oxygenated by the lungs. lymph that is squeezed out of the blood needs to be returned to the heart too, so we need a lymphatic system.
lovely content within the blood will come out of the organs so we musnt forget them.
step 5: fascia
wrap all the muscles up in deep fascia so theyre extra stretchy and bouncy.
step 6: fat
cover everything in a fatty layer, and in that fatty layer we have blood vessels which will spread thin into capilliaries which will find the fascia and epimysium and allow blood to pour over the muscles.
step 7: skin
cover everything with skin and hair and a microbiome.
