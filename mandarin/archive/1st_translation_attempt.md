1 Yinwei "God", "the universal setpiece" zai buduan bianhua.
1 because of god, the universal setpiece is constantly changing

2 "God" shi yi zhong yali. Zhe zhong yali zai shuo: "Wo jiao ni zuo shenme, ni jiu zuo shenme. Fouze, wo hui huimie ni."
2 god is a pressure. this pressure is continually saying: ""

3 "God" de yali zuoyong yu "the universal setpiece". Shijie zai "the universal setpiece" zhizhong. "the verdant maw" qi zai shijie zhishang.
3 god's pressure enacts upon the universal setpiece. the world sits within the universal setpiece. the verdant straddles the world. 

4 "God" de yali zuowei taiyang xiang shijie xianxian. Taiyang shi "God" de yali de xianxian, wei shijie, dan ye wei "the verdant maw" — yinwei "the verdant maw" qi zai shijie zhishang.
4 god's pressure manifests towards the sun (taiyang). the sun is god's pressure manifested, for the world, but also for the verdant maw - since the verdant maw straddles the world.

5 "the verdant maw" baohan shijie zhong de suoyou shengming. Taiyang xiang "the verdant maw" shixing "God" de yizhi, shuo: '"verdant maw", wo jiao ni zuo shenme, ni jiu zuo shenme. Fouze, wo hui huimie ni.' Na yi xiang yangguang zhilei de xingshi xianxian.
5 the verdant maw contains all life within the world. the sun enacts gods will, saying 'verdant maw, do what i say or i will destroy you'. which manifests as sunrays

6 "the verdant maw" huiying zhe jian shi, yi weiyi neng zuo de fangshi: tongguo "hypercashing". Fouze, ta hui bei huimie. Dang ta zai "hypercashing" de shihou, ta zai buduan gaibian ta de xingtai. Na jiu shi women kandao de dongxi, dang women kandao dongzhiwu shengming de dongtai. Dongzhiwu shengming de dongtai, jiu shi "the verdant maw" youyu "hypercashing" er gaibian de guocheng.


7 Renlei yu "the verdant maw" gongcun. Tamen ye keyi xiang gan ma jiu gan ma. Dan ruguo tamen bu "hypercash", tamen hui jingyou taiyang, zaoshou laizi "God" de feilai henghuo ban de huimie. Dan tamen xuyao "hypercash", duikang suoyou huimie. Yinwei renlei ye miandui laizi qita renlei de huimie. Haiyou "the verdant maw". Suoyi, ruguo tamen bu xiang bei huimie, tamen jiu xuyao "hypercash".

8 Youxie renlei hen chun, tamen renwei, weile baohu ziji mianyü huimie, tamen bixu "xiang de bi huimie geng yuan", huozhe "meng de bi huimie geng da". Bingqie yong cailiao huo jihua lai fanji ta. Na jiu shi ziben zhuyi de jiben yuanze. Ta hen chun, yinwei ruguo ni zhen de xiang baohu ziji mianyü huimie, ni zhi xuyao "hypercash".

9 Guanyu shenxue zhiliang, zuowei renlei, women tongguo renxing lai lijie shijie, suoyi women zhi neng ba zhiliang lijie wei haoxiang ta shi yi ge ren. Zhe jiu shi weishenme zhuiqiu jinqian xing bu tong, yinwei jinqian bu tai xiang ren. Shengming fan'er shi guanyu lijie zhiliang de xingge: nage ren; shei shi ni de shenxue zhiliang, he ni shengming de zhongxin. Suizhe ni chengzhang, zhujian liaojie ziji, ni hui lijie renxing, bingqie jieshi guanyu ni shenxue zhiliang de renxing de zhenli.
